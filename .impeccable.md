## Design Context

### Users
A family of four kids (Ava, Ben, Chloe, Dylan) who check a TRMNL e-ink display each morning to see their chores, a daily scripture, and vocabulary words to sneak into conversation. The display is a shared household fixture — glanced at quickly, not studied.

### Brand Personality
**Warm, playful, encouraging.** This is a family bulletin board with heart — not a task tracker. The Word Sneak game and daily scripture give it personality beyond a chore list. It should feel like something the family *wants* to check each morning.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Reference:** Kindle/e-reader — clean typography, comfortable reading, content-first
- **Anti-references:** Cluttered bulletin boards (every element earns its space), boring plain text (needs visual structure and hierarchy)
- **Theme:** Black and white only (e-ink hardware constraint), high-contrast, typography-driven
- **Fonts:** Arial/Helvetica sans-serif for e-ink legibility; Geist for web UI

### Emotional Goal
**Curiosity and delight.** The kids should feel drawn to check the display — the scripture and Word Sneak words make it interesting, not just obligatory. The chores are clear, but the surrounding content makes the whole thing feel like a daily surprise.

### Design Principles

1. **Every element earns its space.** On a 800x480 e-ink display, there's no room for decoration. If it's on screen, it serves a purpose — but "delight" is a valid purpose.

2. **Typography is the design system.** With only black and white available, hierarchy comes entirely from font size, weight, spacing, and case. Use these deliberately.

3. **Scannable first, readable second.** Kids glance at their name, scan their chores, then optionally read the scripture and words. Design for the glance, reward the read.

4. **Warm structure, not cold grids.** The layout should feel organized but approachable — like a well-designed children's book page, not a spreadsheet.

5. **Surprise within consistency.** The layout is predictable (kids know where to look), but the content rotates daily (scripture, words). The design should support both stability and freshness.

### Hardware Constraints
- 4 layout variants: Full (800x480), Half-Horizontal (800x240), Half-Vertical (400x480), Quadrant (400x240)
- Black and white only — no grayscale on e-ink
- Rendered as inline HTML/CSS strings, not React components
- Must be legible at e-ink resolution and refresh rate
